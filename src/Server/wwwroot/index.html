ROOMLY GUIDE

How can we help?

Booking basics, live updates, roles, AI and API reference.

Getting started

Create an account or sign in. Choose an active room, select a date and use an available slot to make a booking.

Booking a room

Rooms show their available time slots. Booking is confirmed only after the server saves it. Your bookings are tied to your account.

Booking conflicts

One time slot can have at most one booking. If another person wins the simultaneous request, you receive a conflict (HTTP 409); refresh the schedule and pick another slot.

Realtime updates

When a booking is saved, SignalR notifies people viewing that room. The schedule updates without a page refresh; reconnect status appears at the top.

User roles

Users can view rooms and schedules, book slots and view their own bookings. Admins can also manage rooms, availability and all bookings.

Autonomous AI booking

In AI Assistant, start with a direct command such as “Book Cedar on September 29 at 10:00 AM.” Include the exact room, date, and start time. Times use your browser's time zone unless you specify another one. The assistant asks a follow-up instead of booking when key details are unclear. A successful AI booking appears in My Bookings and is labeled “Booked by AI”. Read the AI booking guide.

Availability suggestions

Questions such as “Which rooms are free tomorrow?” do not create bookings. If the assistant suggests a slot, review it and select Confirm booking. If no exact future slot matches your request or another user books it first, no booking is created; choose another available slot.

Cancel a booking

Open My Bookings and select Cancel booking beside one of your future bookings. You cannot cancel another user's booking or a slot that has already started. The room schedule updates for active viewers automatically. AI cannot cancel bookings on your behalf.

AI availability and privacy

The assistant requires an authenticated account and a configured AI provider. If it is unavailable, room search and manual booking still work. The assistant receives only the room and booking details needed for your request; it cannot run SQL or manage another user's bookings.

AI Skills

Administrators can draft, generate and upload skills, then review and activate them. Skills are untrusted text guidance; they cannot run code or access the database.

API & architecture
